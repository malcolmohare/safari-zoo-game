🦒 Safari Zoo: 2.5D look samples

Mock-ups only, not playable. They use the same animal art as the game, drawn in an isometric (and one ¾ top-down) view. Back to the game

1 · Savanna safari (isometric)

The jeep chases a herd across the grass. Trees, rocks and the water hole get height and shadows, and a net is in flight.

2 · Your zoo (isometric)

Fenced pens with decorations, the office, the garage and paths, with the keeper walking around.

3 · Tundra on a raised plateau (isometric)

Snow, ice and pines, with stepped terrain to show off real elevation.

4 · Jungle (¾ top-down, "2.5D")

Same objects, but the camera looks straight north at an angle instead of from the corner. It's a gentler change from the current game and easier to steer with a joystick.

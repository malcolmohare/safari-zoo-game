🦒 Safari Zoo: handheld pixel-art samples

Mock-ups only, not playable. Each scene is drawn at real handheld resolution (240×160 like the Game Boy Advance, or 160×144 like the Game Boy Color) and scaled up with chunky pixels. The animals are the game's current art, shrunk and given a dark outline. In a real version they'd be hand-drawn pixel sprites. Back to the game

1 · Savanna safari (GBA style)

Tile map, round clumpy trees, a hearts HUD for jeep health, net ammo on the B button, and money as rupees.

2 · Your zoo (GBA style)

Fenced pens, a village-style office, paths and flowers, and a dialog box.

3 · Tundra with cliffs (GBA style)

Stepped snowy ledges with cliff faces, pines, and a frozen lake.

4 · Jungle (Game Boy Color style)

Lower resolution and a reduced colour palette, with the HUD in a bar at the bottom.
